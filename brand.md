# Brand — Cortexel

_Status: accepted mark v2 · 2026-08-20_

Cortexel's identity is the **evidence fold**: caller-declared neural data crosses
a fail-closed authorship and validation boundary and becomes one deterministic,
inspectable figure artifact. The mark encodes the contract, not a charting UI.

## Mark anatomy

- The magenta voxel is a caller-declared neural population. Its white seed point
  is one neuron. Both remain visibly outside Cortexel's authority boundary.
- The open white bracket is the strict caller-authority, structural, semantic and
  scientific validation boundary. It is not a checkmark or a claim that arbitrary
  input passes.
- The open, C-shaped magenta frame is the Cortexel-authored artifact. Its shape is
  both a folded output boundary and an ownable initial without using a wordmark.
- The white trace is the deterministic figure compiled inside that artifact. It
  is deliberately neutral so contract-selected scientific palettes retain their
  meaning.
- The lower terminal rail represents the returned inspectable value and disclosure
  surface; the white point represents the current artifact binding and provenance
  digest. It does not claim a canonical row-byte sidecar or table-cell binding that
  the implementation does not yet provide.

The invariant is deliberately reducible to:

```text
declared neural data  ->  fail-closed boundary  ->  digest-bound figure artifact
```

There is no literal dashboard, axis chrome, table grid, shield, brain silhouette,
checkmark or certification seal. Those motifs either collapse at small sizes or
misstate what the library owns.

## Motion contract

The loop illustrates one valid request only. A population token reaches and stops
at the boundary, the bracket scans, the deterministic trace resolves, and the
artifact digest pulses. The animation must never imply that every request passes,
that Cortexel authored the caller's declarations, or that a rendered figure proves
scientific truth.

The non-animated and reduced-motion state is the complete evidence fold, never an
empty loading state.

## Geometry and reduction

The core silhouette is **voxel + bracket + open C**. Keep those three masses and
their left-to-right authorship order intact. The trace and digest may be simplified
at very small sizes, but the boundary must remain open and the caller-owned voxel
must never move inside it in the static mark.

The design is tuned to remain recognizable at 24–32 px, to match the machined
standalone project badges in the portfolio graph, and to work without a wordmark.

## Color governance

Brand magenta, violet, white and black are identity chrome only. They must never
replace or reinterpret the contract-selected scientific palettes in
`core/colormaps.ts`.

Scientific color remains data-semantic and fail-closed:

- Crameri palettes remain the source of truth for quantitative fields.
- Signed fields continue to use the declared diverging map.
- Categorical identity never relies on hue alone.
- Rainbow/jet defaults and arbitrary caller CSS remain out of contract.

Typography and host UI remain host-owned; this logo does not invent an app-level
theme or global CSS surface for the library.

## Delivery and accessibility

The canonical README assets are `assets/logo-dark.svg` and
`assets/logo-light.svg`, exported from the same portfolio generator. The mark is
transparent, theme-explicit through `<picture>`, accessible by a concise textual
description, free of external resources and scripts, and complete when animation
is unavailable.
